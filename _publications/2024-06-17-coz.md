## Abstract

Most current arbitrary-scale image super-resolution (SR) methods have commonly relied on simulated data generated by simple synthetic degradation models (e.g., bicubic downsampling) at continuous various scales, thereby falling short in capturing the complex degradation of real-world images. This limitation hinders the visual quality of these methods when applied to real-world images. To address this issue, we propose the Continuous Optical Zooming dataset (COZ), by constructing an automatic imaging system to collect images at fine-grained various focal lengths within a specific range and providing strict image pair alignment. The COZ dataset serves as a benchmark to provide real-world data for training and testing arbitrary-scale SR models. To enhance the model's robustness against real-world image degradation, we propose a Local Mix Implicit network (LMI) based on the MLP-mixer architecture and meta-learning, which directly learns the local texture information by simultaneously mixing features and coordinates of multiple independent points. The extensive experiments demonstrate the superior performance of the arbitrary-scale SR models trained on the COZ dataset compared to models trained on simulated data. Our LMI model exhibits the superior effectiveness compared to other models. This study is of great significance in developing more efficient algorithms and improving the performance of arbitrary-scale image SR methods in practical applications. Our dataset and codes are available at [https://github.com/pf0607/COZ](https://github.com/pf0607/COZ).

## 摘要

现有任意尺度图像超分辨率方法通常依赖简单合成退化（如 bicubic 下采样）生成的模拟数据，难以刻画真实图像的复杂退化，从而限制了其在真实场景中的视觉质量。为此，我们构建连续光学变焦数据集 COZ：通过自动成像系统在特定焦距范围内采集细粒度焦距图像，并提供严格的图像对对齐。COZ 可作为真实世界任意尺度超分模型的训练与测试基准。为提升模型对真实退化的鲁棒性，我们提出基于 MLP-mixer 与元学习的局部混合隐式网络 LMI，通过同时混合多个独立点的特征与坐标来直接学习局部纹理。实验表明，在 COZ 上训练的任意尺度超分模型优于模拟数据训练的模型，且 LMI 优于其他对比方法。数据集与代码见 [https://github.com/pf0607/COZ](https://github.com/pf0607/COZ)。
