## Abstract

Virtual production with large-scale digital screens has revolutionized filmmaking. However, the smooth glassy surfaces of the screens, coupled with complex camera–display interactions, introduce challenging degradations, i.e., screen reflections and moiré patterns, which severely compromise visual quality. Existing approaches for demoiréing and reflection removal fall short in this context, as they treat these degradations in isolation. To address this challenge, we present the Screen Degradation Dataset (SDD), the real-world dataset that jointly captures both degradations. We further propose the Reflection-Moiré Complement Network (RMCNet), which integrates a Reflection Separation Module (RSM) to disentangle reflection and transmission layers and a Reflection-Moiré Complement Module (RMCM) to exploit complementary cues between the two degradation types. Extensive experiments demonstrate that RMCNet surpasses state-of-the-art methods in both quantitative and qualitative evaluations.

## 摘要

大规模数字屏幕虚拟制片正在改变电影制作方式。然而，屏幕光滑的玻璃表面以及复杂的相机–显示交互会引入屏幕反射和摩尔纹等退化，严重损害画面质量。现有去摩尔纹与反射去除方法通常将两类退化孤立处理，难以应对这一场景。为此，我们提出屏幕退化数据集 SDD，这是同时捕获两类真实退化的数据集，并进一步提出反射-摩尔纹互补网络 RMCNet：其中反射分离模块（RSM）用于分解反射层与透射层，反射-摩尔纹互补模块（RMCM）用于挖掘两类退化之间的互补信息。大量实验表明，RMCNet 在定量与定性评价上均优于现有方法。
